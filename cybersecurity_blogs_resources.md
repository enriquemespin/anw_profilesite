## 💡 Tips for Using These Resources

1. **RSS Feeds:** Most of these blogs offer RSS feeds for easy aggregation
2. **Twitter/X:** Many security researchers share breaking news on social media
3. **Newsletters:** Sign up for daily/weekly newsletters from sources like Krebs, Dark Reading, and The Hacker News
4. **Podcasts:** Many of these sources also have podcast counterparts
5. **Conferences:** DEF CON, Black Hat, RSA Conference — many speakers publish their research online
